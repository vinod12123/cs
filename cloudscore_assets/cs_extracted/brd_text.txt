4. Detailed Use Cases





CloudScore
 ASTRA ships ten primary use cases across three capability tiers. Each use case maps directly to a REI agent capability or a new ASTRA-native AI agent. All use cases are designed to deliver value within the first 30 minutes of onboarding.


TIER 
1  |
  Autonomous Incident Intelligence


UC-
01  Live
 Incident Dollar Meter

Persona: On-Call SRE + FinOps Lead

Problem: When a P0 incident fires at 2 AM, teams know the service is down — but no one knows the real-time revenue and cloud cost impact. Financial post-mortems happen days later, too late to influence decisions during the incident.

AI SRE Solution: ASTRA's REI agent activates a Live Incident Dollar Meter the moment an alert fires. Using the 
CloudScore
 cost engine, it calculates: (a) cloud spend burning per minute due to cascading over-provisioning or retry storms, (b) estimated revenue impact from SLO breach, and (c) customer-facing cost if SLA credits are triggered. The meter is displayed live in Slack, the ASTRA dashboard, and the incident timeline.
Business Value: Reduces mean time to financial escalation by 90%. Enables faster executive briefings. Creates direct accountability between SRE actions and CFO-level outcomes.


UC-
02  Autonomous
 Root Cause Analysis (Multi-Signal)

Persona: On-Call SRE / L1 Engineer

Problem: Investigating an incident requires manually switching between Datadog, CloudWatch, Grafana, and Kibana — correlating thousands of signals across logs, metrics, and traces. Mean time to root cause (MTTRC) averages 45–90 minutes for experienced engineers, and much longer for L1 on-call.

AI SRE Solution: ASTRA deploys five specialized AI agents in parallel: Metrics Intelligence Agent, Log Analysis Agent, Deployment Correlation Agent, Infrastructure State Agent, and Request Trace Agent. Each agent follows an autonomous hypothesis-and-test loop, surfacing ranked RCA candidates with evidence chains. The Knowledge Retrieval Agent cross-references historical incidents and runbooks. A unified RCA report with a confidence score and recommended next action is available within 2–5 minutes.
Business Value: Reduces MTTRC from 45–90 minutes to under 5 minutes. Frees L1 engineers from manual correlation. Generates shareable evidence chains for post-mortems automatically.


UC-
03  Cross
-Cloud Financial Cascade Graph

Persona: FinOps Lead + VP Engineering

Problem: An incident in one cloud region often triggers cost cascades in another — a DynamoDB throttle in us-east-1 causes Lambda retries that spike GCP 
BigQuery
 export costs. These cross-cloud financial cascades are invisible to single-cloud tools and difficult to untangle post-incident.

AI SRE Solution: ASTRA's Graph Intelligence Agent builds a real-time Cross-Cloud Financial Cascade Graph using Neo4j, correlating topology changes, cost anomalies, and incident signals across AWS, Azure, GCP, and OCI simultaneously. It identifies the financial blast radius of each incident node, showing which services are burning money downstream and in which cloud.
Business Value: Identifies hidden cost cascades within minutes. Enables prioritization of remediation steps by financial impact, not just technical severity. Creates a defensible post-mortem artifact for finance and engineering leadership.


TIER 
2  |
  Proactive & Predictive AI SRE


UC-
04  Pre
-Deploy Economic Risk Score (PERS)

Persona: DevOps / Release Engineer + Engineering Manager

Problem: Every deployment is a reliability and cost risk. Teams deploy changes without knowing whether the new build will increase error rates, burn through error budget, or cause a 40% cost spike due to inefficient queries or missing cache warm-up. Rollback decisions happen reactively, after damage is done.

AI SRE Solution: Before any deployment merges to production, ASTRA computes a Pre-Deploy Economic Risk Score (PERS) by combining: (a) historical cost deltas for similar deployments, (b) SLO burn rate projections based on error pattern matching, (c) infrastructure topology impact analysis via the 
CloudScore
 graph engine, and (d) LLM-based code diff analysis for known cost anti-patterns (N+1 queries, missing pagination, unthrottled SDK calls). PERS is surfaced as a CI/CD gate in GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins.
Business Value: Prevents costly deployments before they reach production. Reduces deployment-induced incident rate by an estimated 35–55%. Creates a data-driven deployment risk culture without slowing release velocity.


UC-
05  Error
 Budget Bank with Cost Reserves

Persona: Engineering Manager + SRE Lead

Problem: Standard SLO tooling shows error budget burn rate as a percentage or time remaining. But there is no connection between error budget consumption and the financial cost of that unreliability — SLA penalties, wasted cloud retries, and customer churn all carry real dollar values that are hidden from SRE dashboards.

AI SRE Solution: ASTRA's Error Budget Bank extends SLO tracking with a parallel financial reserve layer. Each service's error budget has a corresponding Cost Reserve — the estimated cloud spend available before the budget is exhausted. As reliability events burn error budget, ASTRA shows both the time reserve and the financial reserve depleting in real time. Alerts fire when the cost reserve crosses configurable thresholds ($500, $2K, $10K). Budget re-allocation recommendations are generated automatically.
Business Value: Creates a shared language between SRE and finance teams. Enables engineering managers to quantify reliability investments in CFO terms. Supports FinOps allocation of reliability costs to specific teams and products.

-Powered Observability with Natural Language Query

Persona: Any Engineer / On-Call SRE

Problem: Writing 
PromQL
,

, KQL, or CloudWatch Insights queries requires deep expertise. On-call engineers at 3 AM should not be debugging query syntax — they should be fixing the system. Existing AI assistants like Datadog Bits AI are locked to a single vendor's ecosystem and cannot query multi-cloud telemetry.

AI SRE Solution: ASTRA provides a unified natural language observability interface — 'Ask ASTRA' — that translates plain English questions into the appropriate telemetry query for the correct backend (Prometheus, Loki, CloudWatch, Azure Monitor, GCP Cloud Logging, Datadog, or New Relic). Answers include auto-generated charts, anomaly annotations, and relevant historical context. The interface is available in the ASTRA dashboard, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and via API.
Business Value: Democratizes observability across all engineering levels. Reduces query time from minutes to seconds. Accelerates onboarding of junior engineers to on-call rotations.

UC-
08  Autonomous
 Incident Remediation with Runbook Execution

Persona: On-Call SRE / Platform Team

Problem: Even when root cause is identified quickly, executing the remediation requires manual steps — restarting pods, updating config maps, scaling ASGs, flushing caches, rolling back deployments. In high-pressure incident situations, manual execution introduces errors and delays that extend MTTR unnecessarily.

AI SRE Solution: ASTRA's Execution Agent maintains a library of parameterized runbooks (AWS SSM Automation, Kubernetes 
kubectl
, Terraform, Ansible, and custom shell scripts). Upon RCA confirmation, ASTRA proposes an execution plan with the specific commands and parameters to resolve the incident. With user approval (or fully autonomously in Auto-Pilot mode), ASTRA executes the runbook, monitors the recovery signal, and rolls back if the remediation makes the situation worse. All actions are logged to an immutable audit trail.
Business Value: Reduces median MTTR by 60–75% for known incident patterns. Eliminates manual execution errors during high-stress incidents. Enables 'lights out' remediation for Tier-3 low-risk incidents, allowing on-call engineers to sleep.


UC-
09  Reliability
 ROI Command Center

Persona: CTO / VP Engineering / FinOps Lead

Problem: Engineering leadership cannot easily quantify the ROI of SRE investments. The cost of incidents (cloud waste, SLA credits, engineering time) is scattered across billing consoles, PagerDuty reports, and spreadsheets. Boards and CFOs ask hard questions that SRE teams cannot answer with data.

AI SRE Solution: ASTRA's Reliability ROI Command Center aggregates: (a) total cost of incidents over any 
time period
, (b) cost savings from autonomous remediations, (c) SLA credit exposure avoided, (d) engineering hours saved via automation, and (e) cloud waste eliminated by ASTRA recommendations. The dashboard produces CFO-ready reports with trend lines, peer benchmarks, and projected annual savings. A 'Reliability ROI' metric ($ saved / $ invested in SRE tooling) is calculated monthly.
Business Value: Provides a board-ready data story for SRE investment decisions. Justifies ASTRA's own cost. Enables FinOps and SRE teams to present a unified reliability + cost narrative to executive leadership.


UC-
10  Institutional
 Memory & Incident Knowledge Graph

Persona: Senior SRE / Engineering Lead

Problem: Every incident generates valuable institutional knowledge — but that knowledge lives in Slack threads, Confluence pages, and individual engineers' heads. When the engineer who solved the last database deadlock leaves the company, that knowledge leaves too. Recurring incidents take just as long to resolve the second and third time.

AI SRE Solution: ASTRA builds a persistent Incident Knowledge Graph (Neo4j-backed) that captures: RCA findings, remediation steps taken, who executed them, how long recovery took, and the financial impact. Every future incident is automatically matched against this graph. When a similar incident fires, ASTRA presents the historical playbook with similarity score, time-to-resolution benchmark, and the cost impact of following vs. deviating from the historical approach.
Business Value: Reduces time-to-resolution for repeat incidents by 70–85%. Reduces dependency on individual 'hero' engineers. Creates a self-improving SRE system that gets faster and more accurate over time.
